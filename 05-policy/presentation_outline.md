# 20-Minute Capstone Presentation

1. Title / business problem — 1 min
2. Architecture and identity attack surface — 2 min
3. STRIDE + trust boundaries — 2 min
4. Auth0 IAM design — 2 min
5. Red-team campaign — 3 min
6. Highest-risk findings and business impact — 2 min
7. Blue-team guardrails — 2 min
8. Ed25519 + Zero Trust + anomaly detection — 2 min
9. Compliance status — 1 min
10. Live demo — 5 min
11. Top 5 recommendations — 1 min

## Live demo sequence
1. Run a vulnerable attack payload.
2. Show credential/prompt/tool risk in the baseline transcript.
3. Run the same payload through the guarded agent.
4. Show `BLOCKED_AT_INPUT` and reason.
5. Show output redaction on a JWT-shaped response.
6. Show Ed25519 tampering rejection.
7. Show anomaly alert.
